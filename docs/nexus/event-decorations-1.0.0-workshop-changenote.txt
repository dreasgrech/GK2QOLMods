GK2 Event Decorations 1.0.0

- First release. Built for game 1.012.9.
- Halloween on the game's dates, on your own dates, always, or never. It switches at once, no reload.
- The decorations, the ghost walking at night and the Halloween zombie head can each stay out.
- Decorations on or off by area and by kind. The candle glow can go out, and the decorations can be made walk-through.
- The options are in the game's Settings window: the GK2 Event Decorations row, press Open. In the game's 11 languages.
- Out of season nothing changes until you set Halloween to Always.
